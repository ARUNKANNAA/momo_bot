1. Getting started

Momo is an expressive desktop companion robot built with an ESP8266 Wemos D1 mini and a 0.96" I2C OLED screen. It reacts dynamically to your touch, shows ambient moods, and displays live synchronized time and weather.

Power on and off

Slide the miniature power switch to turn Momo on or off. The switch is wired between the TP4056 output and the D1 mini 5V pin, safely cutting battery power to the microcontroller.

Charging the battery

Plug a standard USB-C cable into the TP4056 charger board. The 3.7V 500mAh LiPo battery charges safely even when Momo's power switch is turned off.

⚠️ Important Safety Warning: Do not plug USB power into the Wemos D1 mini board while the battery is connected to its 5V pin. Always charge through the TP4056 USB-C port only.

Power-on sequence

  1. SEARCHING... with animated dots and your saved Wi-Fi name for up to 15 seconds while connecting.
  2. Animated eyes appear with natural blinking and wander gaze. Momo is ready!

If Momo has no saved network or cannot reach it, it automatically opens the setup screen. See below.

2. First-time Wi-Fi setup

Momo connects to Wi-Fi to synchronize real-time clock via NTP and fetch current weather from Open-Meteo. Wi-Fi credentials are saved in EEPROM so Momo remembers them across power cycles.

When Momo has no network saved or cannot connect, it broadcasts its own setup hotspot:

WiFi SSIDMomo-Setup Password12345678 Portal IP192.168.4.1
  1. On your smartphone or PC, go to Wi-Fi settings and connect to Momo-Setup with password 12345678.
  2. Open browser and go to http://192.168.4.1. The Momo setup page opens.
  3. Enter your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi Name and Password.
  4. Click Detect My Location to auto-fill your exact latitude and longitude for weather.
  5. Click SAVE & CONNECT. Momo saves credentials to EEPROM, restarts, and connects.
🌐 Open Momo Setup IP (http://192.168.4.1)

💡 Note: Momo works only on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks (ESP8266 does not support 5 GHz). You can also view the exact setup file at setup.html!

3. Touch controls

Momo uses a TTP223 capacitive touch module hidden beneath its head enclosure. Touch it with quick taps or long holds:

Screen / ModeTouch ActionResult
Eyes (Normal)Single tap (< 0.5s)Happy face for 5 seconds
Eyes (Normal)Triple tap quickly (< 0.7s apart)Angry face for 5 seconds
Eyes (Any)Long press (5 seconds)Opens Time screen
Time ScreenSingle tapOpens Weather screen
Time ScreenLong press (5 seconds)Back to Eyes
Weather ScreenLong press (5 seconds)Back to Eyes

Touch Tips

  • For the angry face, tap in rapid succession (under 0.7 seconds between taps).
  • A hold shorter than 5 seconds but longer than 0.5 seconds is ignored as an accidental contact.
  • The Time and Weather screens automatically return to Eyes after 10 seconds of inactivity.

5. Time and weather

Time Screen

Hold the touch sensor for 5 seconds from the eyes. Momo displays the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM) with the date below (DD/MM/YYYY). In the default firmware, time is configured for Indian Standard Time (UTC+5:30) via NTP.

Weather Screen (As coded in Arduino)

Single tap on the time screen. Momo fetches free real-time weather from Open-Meteo based on the latitude & longitude saved during setup:

  • WEATHER (header at top)
  • 28.5 C (temperature with one decimal place)
  • Cloudy (condition text mapped from WMO code: Clear, Cloudy, Overcast, Fog, Drizzle, Rain, Snow, Showers, Storm)
  • Humidity 72% (relative humidity percentage)

Weather data automatically refreshes in the background every 10 minutes.

6. Offline mode

If Wi-Fi disconnects or is unavailable, Momo gracefully transitions to offline mode without freezing:

  • All touch reactions, animated eyes, happy, and angry expressions continue working.
  • The weather screen displays OFFLINE and No internet.
  • Momo automatically attempts to quietly reconnect in the background every 60 seconds. When Wi-Fi recovers, the clock and weather automatically resume.

7. Changing Wi-Fi later

To connect Momo to a new Wi-Fi network:

  1. Turn off Momo using the slide switch.
  2. Move Momo out of range of your old Wi-Fi, or temporarily turn off your old router.
  3. Turn Momo on. After 15 seconds of searching, Momo automatically re-opens the Momo-Setup AP.
  4. Connect to Momo-Setup and submit your new Wi-Fi credentials at 192.168.4.1.

Screen stays blankWiring / Power issueVerify battery charge and slide switch. Check OLED wiring: SDA to D2, SCL to D1, VCC to 3V3, GND to G. Ensure I2C address is 0x3C.
Stuck on Setup screenAwaiting configurationConnect phone to Momo-Setup, open 192.168.4.1, and submit your Wi-Fi details.
Cannot open 192.168.4.1Mobile data interferenceTurn off cellular mobile data on your phone while connected to Momo-Setup, then open 192.168.4.1 in browser.
Wi-Fi fails to connect5 GHz network / TypoESP8266 only supports 2.4 GHz. Verify your network is 2.4 GHz and check password for typos.
Touch sensor unresponsiveWiring / GroundingCheck TTP223 wiring: I/O to D5, VCC to 3V3, GND to G. Ensure sensor pad is close to outer enclosure plastic.
Random resets or screen flickeringLow battery voltageRecharge battery via TP4056 USB-C port. Check that the 100 µF capacitor is installed across 5V and GND.
